# Wallpaper flight pack 01

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow for Astralith. All scenes
were requested as wide desktop wallpapers with calm central negative space, no
text, logos, watermarks, HUD elements, or interface graphics.

## Ringfall Survey

Near-photoreal frozen-moon landscape overlooking a huge ringed gas giant at
pre-dawn. The planet is weighted toward the right third, with a low horizon,
midnight-blue terrain, lavender rim light, and restrained cyan reflections.

Output: `assets/wallpapers/ringfall-survey.png`

## Aurora Tide

Scientifically grounded alien-ocean nightscape with black volcanic shoreline,
glassy water, cyan aurora, two small moons, distant crystalline ice, and a dark
central sky suitable for translucent desktop surfaces.

Output: `assets/wallpapers/aurora-tide.png`

## Umbra Array

Near-photoreal radio observatory embedded in an ancient black crater during a
total eclipse. The scene uses a restrained amber corona, worn dark metal,
basalt geology, and tiny cold-cyan utility lights, with the observatory and
eclipse held to opposing thirds.

Output: `assets/wallpapers/umbra-array.png`

## SpaceEngine survey policy

User-captured SpaceEngine frames should form a separate Survey collection. Keep
the simulation HUD disabled, compose major bodies away from the center when
possible, and save captures anywhere below `~/Pictures/Wallpapers`; Parallax
already indexes that tree.
